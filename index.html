Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work with all genres?

Yes. I've worked with authors across fiction, nonfiction, romance, mystery, thriller, sci-fi, fantasy, and more. Every plan is built around the book and its readers.

Do you work with self-published and traditionally published authors?

Yes. Both indie and traditionally published authors are welcome. For traditionally published books, I work alongside your publisher's efforts.

Do you guarantee sales or bestseller rankings?

No marketing service can guarantee sales. I focus on clear goals, honest reporting, and campaigns built around your book. Your results depend on many factors, including the book itself.

Do I need an existing audience to start?

No. Many authors I've worked with started with no audience at all. The plan includes building one from the beginning.

How quickly will I see results?

It depends on the service and your book. Ads can show data within days. Newsletter and review growth usually builds over several weeks or months. I'll explain what to expect in your plan.

How will we communicate?

Through email. You'll receive your plan, updates, and reports by email, and you can reply any time with questions.

Romance Author · Launch Campaign

A Debut Launch Built From Scratch

Starting point: A debut release with no existing audience and a small social following.

What we did: Built a six-week pre-launch plan, created a free reader welcome gift to grow the newsletter, ran targeted ads to comparison titles in the same subgenre, and scheduled promotion posts across launch week.

Results: 1,250 copies sold in launch week, 480 new newsletter subscribers, and 35 reviews within 30 days.

Mystery Author · Amazon Ads

Turning Ad Spend Into Ad Return

Starting point: Ads on a backlist title were spending more than they earned.

What we did: Rebuilt keyword and comparison-title targeting, paused underperforming ads, tested new headlines, and adjusted bids weekly based on performance data.

Results: A 62% improvement in ad return within 8 weeks.

Fantasy Author · Newsletter Growth

Growing a Small List Into an Engaged One

Starting point: A small newsletter list with low engagement.

What we did: Created a lead magnet (a free prequel short story), wrote a five-email welcome sequence, and improved the sign-up page with a clear reader benefit.

Results: 312 new subscribers in 10 weeks and a 46% open rate.

Nonfiction Author · Review Outreach

Reaching the Right Reviewers and Podcasts

Starting point: Few reviews after release, with no clear path to reviewers.

What we did: Built a list of reviewers and podcasts that covered the book's topic, wrote personalized pitch emails, and followed up on promising leads.

Results: 18 reviews and 9 podcast appearances within 4 months.

Results vary by book, genre, price, and market conditions. The examples above are real campaigns shared with permission. They reflect specific projects and are not a promise of future results.

About

Hi, I'm David

For five years, I've spent my days helping authors do what they do best: write. I know the frustration of finishing a book you're proud of and then hearing nothing but silence. Many authors tell me they feel like their book is a message in a bottle with no one on the shore. That's why I built Dave's Author Services. I want to take the marketing off your plate and put your book in front of the readers who will love it.

Over the past five years, I've worked with more than 180 authors across every genre, from first-time indie writers to established names. Each one has taught me something new about readers and about what makes a book stand out. My approach is simple: understand your book, understand your readers, and build a plan you can measure.

Before I found book marketing, I spent several years in digital advertising, where I learned to read data and test ideas before committing to them. When a writer friend asked for help with her first launch, I saw that those same skills could help a good book find its audience. I have been hooked ever since. My favorite moment is seeing a first-time author receive a message from a reader they never expected to reach.
